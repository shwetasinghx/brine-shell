Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

This is a starting-point draft covering the data this website actually collects. It is not legal advice. Have it reviewed against India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) and your specific business practices before publishing.

1. Who we are

Brine & Shell ("we", "us", "our") operates brineandshell.com and sells peanut butter products online. You can reach us at support@brineandshell.com or +91 12345 67890.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact form: name, email, phone number, and the message you send us.
  • Newsletter signup: your email address.
  • Orders: name, email, phone number, delivery address, and order details. Payment is processed by Razorpay. We do not see or store your full card, UPI, or bank details.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiries and fulfil orders.
  • To send order confirmations and delivery updates.
  • To send newsletter emails, only if you subscribed, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

4. Who we share it with

We share the minimum information necessary with the service providers who help us run the business: Razorpay (payments), our email delivery provider, and our delivery/courier partners. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order records as long as needed for accounting, tax, and warranty/return purposes. You can ask us to delete contact-form or newsletter data that isn't tied to an order.

6. Your rights

You can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing support@brineandshell.com.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@brineandshell.com.
