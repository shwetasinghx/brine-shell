Refund & Returns Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

This is a starting-point draft matching the "7-day return policy" already mentioned on the Contact page's FAQ. Not legal advice. Confirm the exact terms you want to commit to before publishing, and that they match what Razorpay requires for your account.

1. Damaged or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged, spoiled, or different from what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery at support@brineandshell.com with your order number and a photo of the issue. We'll arrange a replacement or a full refund.

2. Food products and hygiene

Because our products are food items, we can only accept returns for damaged, spoiled, or incorrect items, not for change of mind, once a jar, cube pack, or sachet has been opened.

3. How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method via Razorpay, typically within 5–7 business days of approval.

4. Bulk orders

Bulk orders (20+ units) are handled case by case. Contact us before ordering if you need specific terms.
