Effective Date: 24 September 2026 · Last Updated: 24 September 2026

This Refund, Return & Cancellation Policy applies to purchases made through brineandshell.com from Brine & Shell, operated by BRINE AND SHELL ("Brine & Shell", "we", "us", or "our").

Because our products are packaged food products, returns and refunds are subject to the conditions set out below and to all mandatory rights and remedies available to consumers under applicable Indian law.

1. Food-Safety and Hygiene Policy

For health, hygiene and food-safety reasons, opened, used, tampered-with or partially consumed food products generally cannot be accepted for return.

This restriction does not limit any mandatory statutory rights available to a consumer in relation to a product that is defective, unsafe, contaminated, damaged before delivery, incorrectly supplied, or otherwise not in conformity with the order or applicable law.

2. When We Accept a Return / Replacement / Refund

You may contact us if you receive:

A. A damaged product

The product arrives physically damaged, leaking, broken, crushed or otherwise materially damaged.

B. A wrong product

You received a product, flavour, quantity or variant different from the product ordered.

C. A defective or materially non-conforming product

The product has a material defect or does not conform to the description or applicable product requirements.

D. A product with a verified quality or safety issue

If you believe that the product has a genuine food-safety, contamination, spoilage or other quality issue, immediately stop consuming the product and contact us.

We may request photographs, videos, batch/lot information, packaging details and other information reasonably necessary to investigate the complaint.

Where appropriate, we may request that the product and/or packaging be retained for inspection or investigation.

3. Reporting a Problem and Video Verification

For damaged, incorrect, defective or other eligible refund/return claims, the customer must contact us within 48 hours of receiving the product.

To initiate a refund or replacement request, the customer may be required to submit a clear, continuous video verification through the method specified on the Website.

The video may be required to include:

  • The Order ID
  • Customer name and other order details requested by the Website
  • The product received
  • The outer packaging and shipping label
  • The condition of the product and packaging
  • Batch/Lot number and best-before/expiry information, where applicable
  • Any visible damage, leakage, defect or other issue being reported

The Website may request additional information or evidence reasonably necessary to verify the claim.

For eligible claims, photographs, videos and other supporting evidence may be used to verify the condition of the product and investigate the complaint.

4. Products That Are Generally Not Eligible for Return

Subject to applicable law, we generally do not accept returns merely because:

  • You changed your mind after purchase
  • You ordered the wrong flavour or quantity
  • You do not personally like the taste
  • You do not like the texture that is consistent with the product's disclosed characteristics
  • The product has naturally separated or settled where such separation is consistent with the product's ingredients and has not affected safety or conformity
  • The product has been opened, used or partially consumed
  • The product has been improperly stored after delivery
  • The product was damaged after delivery due to mishandling, exposure to excessive heat, moisture or unsuitable storage
  • The customer provided an incorrect or incomplete delivery address
  • Delivery was unsuccessful because the customer was unavailable or failed to accept the shipment
  • The customer refuses delivery for reasons unrelated to a defect, damage, incorrect product or other legally recognised ground

Nothing in this section excludes a remedy that cannot legally be excluded.

5. Natural Variation in Food Products

Our products may contain natural ingredients and may exhibit reasonable variations in:

  • Colour
  • Aroma
  • Consistency
  • Texture
  • Appearance
  • Oil separation
  • Flavour intensity

Such reasonable variations, where consistent with the product description and applicable food standards, do not automatically constitute a defect.

Always follow the storage and consumption instructions provided on the product packaging.

6. Order Cancellation

Orders may be cancelled only before the order is marked as shipped by contacting us through the support details provided on the Website.

Once an order has been marked as shipped, cancellation may not be possible because the product may already have entered the delivery process.

Where cancellation is accepted before the order is marked as shipped, the eligible refund will generally be processed through the original payment method, subject to applicable payment-provider processing timelines.

If applicable law provides a consumer with a cancellation or refund right that applies notwithstanding this provision, that right will prevail.

7. Refund Process

For an eligible refund request, the customer must submit the required information and, where requested, the required video verification within 48 hours of receiving the product.

Once the claim and supporting information have been reviewed and the refund is approved, we may provide:

  • A full refund
  • A partial refund
  • A replacement product
  • Another remedy appropriate to the circumstances

subject to applicable law.

The appropriate remedy may depend on the nature of the complaint, availability of replacement stock, condition of the product and the consumer's applicable legal rights.

Approved refunds will normally be initiated to the original payment method unless another lawful method is agreed or required.

The time taken for the refund to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment gateway, bank or card issuer and may be outside Brine & Shell's control.

8. Refunds for Failed / Returned Deliveries

If an order is returned to us because of an incorrect address, repeated delivery failure, refusal to accept delivery or another reason attributable to the customer, we may review the circumstances before processing any refund.

Where a refund is legally or commercially approved, reasonable non-recoverable delivery or logistics charges may be deducted where permitted by applicable law and where such charges were properly disclosed.

No deduction will be made where applicable law prohibits it.

9. Promotional Offers

Products purchased using promotional discounts, coupons or special offers remain subject to this Policy.

Where a refund is approved, the refund will be calculated in accordance with the actual amount paid and the applicable terms of the promotion.

Unless specifically stated otherwise, promotional coupon codes or discounts are not refundable as cash.

10. Failed Payment / Duplicate Payment

If your account is debited but an order is not successfully created, please contact us with the transaction details.

Where a payment has genuinely been received by us without a corresponding successful order, we will investigate and process any eligible refund through the applicable payment mechanism.

In the event of duplicate payment for the same order, the duplicate amount will be reviewed and refunded where verified.

DO NOT CONTINUE CONSUMING THE PRODUCT.

Nothing in this Policy limits rights or remedies available under applicable food-safety or consumer-protection law.
